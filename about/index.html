About this site

Doomsday Countdown is a small fan site counting down to Avengers: Doomsday and collecting the facts fans keep searching for: the release date, runtime, cast and what to watch first.

Everything here comes from official announcements and established entertainment press. When something is only a report, we say so. The site has no connection to Marvel Studios or Disney.
