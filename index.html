Why December 18 matters

Avengers: Doomsday was first dated for May 1, 2026. In 2025 Marvel moved it to December 18, 2026, and pushed Avengers: Secret Wars back with it, so the two films could be finished properly. That makes Doomsday the first Avengers team-up since Endgame in 2019, more than seven years later.

The cast is the biggest Marvel has announced. Thor, Sam Wilson's Captain America, Shuri, Ant-Man and Loki share the screen with the Thunderbolts, the Fantastic Four and the original X-Men actors, from Patrick Stewart to Ian McKellen. At the centre of it all is Doctor Doom, the role that brings Robert Downey Jr. back to Marvel in a completely new part.

Catch up before opening day

Disney+ published an official list of 15 movies and series to watch first, starting with X-Men from 2000 and ending with The Fantastic Four: First Steps. Avengers: Doomsday FAQ

When does Avengers: Doomsday come out?

Avengers: Doomsday opens in theaters on December 18, 2026. Marvel first planned it for May 1, 2026, then moved it to December to give the production more time.

How many days until Avengers: Doomsday?

The countdown at the top of this page shows the exact days, hours, minutes and seconds until December 18, 2026, in your own time zone.

Are Avengers: Doomsday tickets on sale?

Yes. Tickets are already on sale at theaters, starting with premium screens and then all theaters. Check your local cinema or ticket app for showtimes.

How long is Avengers: Doomsday?

The runtime reported by The Hollywood Reporter is 165 minutes, which is 2 hours and 45 minutes. Some cinema listings show slightly different times, so treat it as approximate until the final cut is confirmed.

Who plays Doctor Doom?

Robert Downey Jr. plays Victor von Doom. He played Tony Stark / Iron Man in ten MCU films, and this is a completely new character for him.

Who directs Avengers: Doomsday?

Anthony and Joe Russo, who also directed Captain America: The Winter Soldier, Civil War, Infinity War and Endgame. They are directing Avengers: Secret Wars as well.

What should I watch before Avengers: Doomsday?

Disney+ published a 15-title viewing guide, from X-Men (2000) to The Fantastic Four: First Steps. If you are short on time, watch Endgame, Loki, No Way Home, Deadpool & Wolverine, Thunderbolts* and First Steps. The full list is on the watch order page.

Are the X-Men in Avengers: Doomsday?

Yes. Patrick Stewart, Ian McKellen, James Marsden, Rebecca Romijn, Alan Cumming, Kelsey Grammer and Channing Tatum are all in the announced cast.

What comes after Avengers: Doomsday?

Avengers: Secret Wars, also directed by the Russo brothers, is the next Avengers film and is planned for December 2027.
