Cast FAQ

Who is in the Avengers: Doomsday cast?

Marvel announced 27 actors, led by Robert Downey Jr. as Doctor Doom, with Chris Hemsworth, Anthony Mackie, Sebastian Stan, Letitia Wright, Paul Rudd, Florence Pugh, Pedro Pascal, Tom Hiddleston and the original X-Men cast.

Is Chris Evans in Avengers: Doomsday?

His return has been widely reported, but he was not part of Marvel's official cast announcement in March 2025. This page lists only the officially announced actors.

Is Robert Downey Jr. playing Iron Man again?

No. He plays a new character, Victor von Doom. Tony Stark died in Avengers: Endgame.
