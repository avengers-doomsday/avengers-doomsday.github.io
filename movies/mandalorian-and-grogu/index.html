What is The Mandalorian and Grogu about?

The first Star Wars film in theaters since 2019 continues the Disney+ series The Mandalorian. After the fall of the Empire, the New Republic hires Din Djarin and his apprentice Grogu to rescue Rotta the Hutt, son of Jabba, in exchange for information on a target.

Lucasfilm chose to make this film instead of a fourth season of the show, so it is the next chapter of that story on the big screen.

Cast and crew

Pedro Pascal returns as the Mandalorian. Sigourney Weaver joins as a New Republic figure, Jeremy Allen White voices Rotta the Hutt, and Martin Scorsese has a voice role. Jon Favreau directs and co-wrote it with Dave Filoni and Noah Kloor, and Ludwig Göransson is back on music.

Box office and reviews

The film had mixed reviews. Critics liked the action, the score and Pascal, but not the plot. It made about $346 million, the lowest of any live-action Star Wars film, yet it is still widely searched because of Grogu and because it leads straight into Disney+ viewing.
