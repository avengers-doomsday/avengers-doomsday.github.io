What is Scream 7 about?

Sidney Prescott is back. Years after surviving Ghostface, she has a family, and a new killer goes after her daughter Tatum. The film brings the series back to Sidney after she sat out Scream VI.

It went through a lot of changes before filming, including a new director and new lead cast after the original plans fell apart in 2023.

Cast and crew

Neve Campbell returns as Sidney with Courteney Cox, David Arquette and Matthew Lillard. Isabel May plays Tatum, and Mckenna Grace, Anna Camp and Joel McHale join. Kevin Williamson, who wrote the original 1996 Scream, directs for the first time in the series.

Box office and reviews

Critics did not like it, but fans did: it became the highest-grossing Scream film ever, with about $214 million on a $45 million budget. Horror fans keep searching for it as an entry point to the whole series.
