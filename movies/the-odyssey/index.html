What is The Odyssey about?

Christopher Nolan's version of Homer's Odyssey follows Odysseus, king of Ithaca, on his long way home after the Trojan War. Ten years of war are followed by years at sea, and on the way he meets monsters, gods and temptations while his wife Penelope waits for him and suitors take over his house.

Nolan went for a realistic take on Greek myth, citing old epics like Ran and the stop-motion creatures of Ray Harryhausen as inspiration. It is also the first feature shot entirely on IMAX 70 mm film cameras, which is why so many people searched for IMAX showtimes in July.

Cast and crew

Matt Damon plays Odysseus and Anne Hathaway plays Penelope. The ensemble is one of the biggest of the year: Tom Holland, Robert Pattinson, Lupita Nyong'o, Zendaya, Charlize Theron and Samantha Morton all appear. Nolan wrote, directed and produced it with Emma Thomas, and it was filmed in Morocco, Greece, Italy, Scotland, Iceland and Malta.

Box office and reviews

With about $1.80 billion worldwide it is the second biggest film of 2026, Nolan's most successful film by far and the highest-grossing R-rated film ever made. Critics praised the direction and the huge set pieces, while some readers of Homer disliked changes to the story. People still search for it to find out whether it is worth seeing in IMAX and when it will stream.
