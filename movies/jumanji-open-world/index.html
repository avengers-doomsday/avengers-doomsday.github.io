What is Jumanji: Open World about?

The third film in the modern Jumanji series, and the fifth Jumanji overall, brings the players back into the game. As the title suggests, the game has grown into an open world, and the heroes and their avatars have more ground to cover than ever.

Plot details are being kept quiet, but it picks up after Jumanji: The Next Level (2019) and is billed as the final chapter.

Cast and crew

Dwayne Johnson, Jack Black, Kevin Hart and Karen Gillan return as the game avatars, with Alex Wolff, Madison Iseman, Morgan Turner and Ser'Darius Blain as the players. Nick Jonas, Awkwafina and Danny DeVito are back as well. Jake Kasdan directs again and co-wrote the script with Jeff Pinkner and Scott Rosenberg.
