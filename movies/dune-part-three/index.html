What is Dune: Part Three about?

Dune: Part Three adapts Frank Herbert's 1969 novel Dune Messiah. Paul Atreides is now emperor, and the holy war carried out in his name has spread across the universe. He has to deal with the consequences: plots against his throne, a growing insurgency and visions of disaster.

Denis Villeneuve has said this is the final film of his Dune trilogy, closing Paul's story.

Cast and crew

Timothée Chalamet returns as Paul and Zendaya as Chani, with Rebecca Ferguson and Florence Pugh, and Robert Pattinson joins the cast. Villeneuve directs and wrote the script with Brian K. Vaughan. Hans Zimmer composed the score again. Filming took place in Hungary and the United Arab Emirates in 2025.

Release date and what to expect

It opens on December 18, the very same day as Avengers: Doomsday. That clash is the most talked-about box office weekend of the year and the reason so many people are searching for both films right now.
