What is Minions & Monsters about?

The third Minions film goes back to 1927 Hollywood, right at the moment silent films were giving way to talkies. The Minions decide to make a monster movie of their own, with the chaos you would expect.

It is set decades before Gru appears, so you do not need to know the Despicable Me films to enjoy it.

Cast and crew

Pierre Coffin directs and once again voices the Minions. Brian Lynch co-wrote the script. The voice cast adds Trey Parker, Allison Janney, Christoph Waltz, Jesse Eisenberg, Jeff Bridges and Zoey Deutch. John Powell wrote the score.

Box office and reviews

Many critics called it the best film in the franchise since the first Despicable Me. It earned about $530 million, less than earlier Minions films but still a clear hit and one of the ten biggest films of 2026.
