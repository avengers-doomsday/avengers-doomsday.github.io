What is Michael about?

Michael tells the story of Michael Jackson's early life, from the Jackson 5 in the 1960s to the Bad World Tour at the end of the 1980s. It covers the years when he went from child star to the most famous entertainer on the planet, with the music at the centre.

The film stops before the 1990s. A legal clause meant references to the 1993 allegations were removed, the third act was rewritten and reshoots took place in 2025, which is one reason the budget rose to between $155 and $200 million.

Cast and crew

Michael's nephew Jaafar Jackson plays him as an adult in his first film role, and Juliano Valdi plays him as a child. Colman Domingo plays his father Joe Jackson and Nia Long plays his mother Katherine. Miles Teller and Mike Myers have supporting roles. Antoine Fuqua, director of Training Day and The Equalizer, directs from a script by John Logan.

Box office and reviews

Reviews were mixed: critics praised Jaafar Jackson's performance but many called the story too clean. Audiences did not care. Michael grossed about $1.03 billion, becoming the first biopic ever to pass $1 billion and the biggest Lionsgate film in history. A sequel is in development, which keeps searches going.

Where to watch Michael
