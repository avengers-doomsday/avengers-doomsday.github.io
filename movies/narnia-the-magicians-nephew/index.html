What is Narnia: The Magician's Nephew about?

Greta Gerwig's Narnia film adapts The Magician's Nephew, the first book in the story's timeline. It tells how the world of Narnia was created, and how the lamppost, the White Witch and the wardrobe came to exist, long before the Pevensie children arrived.

It is the first new Narnia film since 2010 and the start of Netflix's take on C. S. Lewis's books.

Cast and crew

Greta Gerwig, director of Barbie and Little Women, wrote and directed it. The cast includes David McKenna and Beatrice Campbell as the children, Emma Mackey, Carey Mulligan, Ciarán Hinds, Daniel Craig and Meryl Streep.

Release date and what to expect

It will be the first Netflix film to get a full worldwide theatrical release. The date moved into 2027, so many people are searching to find out when it actually comes out.

Where to watch Narnia: The Magician's Nephew

IMAX previews start on February 10, 2027, the wide theatrical release is on February 12, and it is set to stream on Netflix from April 2, 2027.
