What is Godzilla Minus Zero about?

The sequel to Godzilla Minus One, the 2023 film that won the Oscar for visual effects, is set in 1949. Japan is still rebuilding after the war when a new threat arrives: King Ghidorah, the three-headed monster. Godzilla returns and is drawn into a fight with it.

Like Minus One, it focuses on ordinary people caught up in the disaster, not just the monster battles.

Cast and crew

Takashi Yamazaki wrote and directed it and also led the visual effects, as he did on Minus One. Ryunosuke Kamiki, Minami Hamabe, Hidetaka Yoshioka, Yuki Yamada and Sakura Ando return, joined by Masami Nagasawa and Jun Kunimura. It is the first Japanese production shot for IMAX.

Box office and reviews

It opened this month to strong reviews and records: the biggest opening day for any live-action film in Japan and the best opening weekend ever for a foreign-language live-action film in the US and Canada. Many people are searching for subtitled versus dubbed showtimes.
