What is Moana (live action) about?

The live-action Moana retells the 2016 animated film: a young woman from the island of Motunui sails beyond the reef to find the demigod Maui and restore the heart of Te Fiti, saving her people.

It keeps the songs fans know, with Lin-Manuel Miranda involved as a producer.

Cast and crew

Catherine Laga'aia makes her film debut as Moana. Dwayne Johnson returns as Maui, the role he voiced in the animated films, and Jemaine Clement is back as Tamatoa. Thomas Kail, who directed the filmed version of Hamilton, directs.

Box office and reviews

With a budget of about $250 million it is one of the most expensive films ever made, but it underperformed with about $324 million worldwide and mixed reviews. Families still search for it alongside the animated films on Disney+.

Where to watch Moana (live action)

As a Disney film it streams on Disney+, next to the animated originals.
