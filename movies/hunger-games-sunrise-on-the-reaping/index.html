What is The Hunger Games: Sunrise on the Reaping about?

The next Hunger Games film goes back 24 years before Katniss, to the 50th Hunger Games, the second Quarter Quell. That year twice as many tributes are reaped, and one of them is a young Haymitch Abernathy from District 12, the mentor fans know from the original films.

It is based on Suzanne Collins' 2025 novel and sits between The Ballad of Songbirds and Snakes and The Hunger Games, so it fills in how Haymitch became the man Katniss meets.

Cast and crew

Joseph Zada plays the young Haymitch, Jesse Plemons plays an adult Plutarch Heavensbee, and Ralph Fiennes is President Snow. Glenn Close, Kieran Culkin as Caesar Flickerman, Elle Fanning as Effie Trinket, Mckenna Grace, Maya Hawke, Whitney Peak and Kelvin Harrison Jr. complete the ensemble. Francis Lawrence, who directed four of the earlier films, directs again.

Release date and what to expect

It is the biggest release of November. The film premiered in London on October 28 and opens in the US on November 20, so searches for tickets, the cast and the book are climbing fast this month.
