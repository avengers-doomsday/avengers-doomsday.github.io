What is Toy Story 5 about?

Two years after Toy Story 4, Bonnie has a new favourite toy, and it is not a toy at all. Lilypad is a tablet, and Woody, Buzz, Jessie and the rest suddenly have to compete with a screen. The story puts Jessie at the front this time, as the toys try to work out where they fit in a child's life when an app can do everything they can.

It is the most modern subject the series has tackled, and it is aimed as much at parents as at kids.

Cast and crew

Andrew Stanton, who co-wrote every earlier Toy Story film and directed Finding Nemo and WALL-E, directs and co-wrote the script with Kenna Harris. Tom Hanks, Tim Allen and Joan Cusack return as Woody, Buzz and Jessie, with Conan O'Brien, Greta Lee and Craig Robinson voicing new characters. Randy Newman wrote the score again, and Taylor Swift contributed the song I Knew It, I Knew You.

Box office and reviews

Toy Story 5 made about $1.15 billion worldwide, the best result in the series and Pixar's biggest opening weekend ever. Reviews were positive, praising the humour and animation. It is the third biggest film of 2026, and families keep searching for it as it heads to streaming.

Where to watch Toy Story 5

As a Disney and Pixar film, Toy Story 5 is headed to Disney+, after a period of digital rental and purchase.
