What is The Super Mario Galaxy Movie about?

The sequel to The Super Mario Bros. Movie takes Mario, Luigi and Princess Peach into space. Bowser is back, this time with his son Bowser Jr., and the heroes have to find and rescue Rosalina, the guardian of the cosmos from the Super Mario Galaxy games.

The film borrows heavily from Super Mario Galaxy and Super Mario Galaxy 2 on the Wii, plus nods to other Nintendo games such as Star Fox, so a lot of the searches come from players looking for the references.

Cast and crew

Chris Pratt, Anya Taylor-Joy, Charlie Day, Jack Black and Keegan-Michael Key return as Mario, Peach, Luigi, Bowser and Toad. New voices include Brie Larson as Rosalina, Benny Safdie as Bowser Jr., and Donald Glover and Glen Powell. Aaron Horvath and Michael Jelenic direct again from a script by Matthew Fogel.

Box office and reviews

Critics were lukewarm, but it still made about $1.01 billion on a $110 million budget, the second biggest video game film ever behind the first Mario movie. It remains one of the most searched family films of the year.

Where to watch The Super Mario Galaxy Movie

Like the first Mario film, it follows the Universal pattern in the US: digital rental first, then Peacock, then Netflix later.
