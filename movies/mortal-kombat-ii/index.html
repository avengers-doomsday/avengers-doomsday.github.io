What is Mortal Kombat II about?

The sequel to the 2021 film finally stages the Mortal Kombat tournament. Martial arts movie star Johnny Cage and the Edenian princess Kitana fight in an interdimensional contest to stop Shao Kahn, the emperor of Outworld.

It leans further into the game lore than the first film, which is what fans asked for.

Cast and crew

Karl Urban joins as Johnny Cage and Adeline Rudolph as Kitana, with Martyn Ford as Shao Kahn. Jessica McNamee, Josh Lawson, Ludi Lin, Hiroyuki Sanada and Joe Taslim return. Simon McQuoid directs again from a script by Jeremy Slater.

Box office and reviews

Reviews were mixed, though most said it improved on the first film. It earned about $130 million on an $80 million budget and is mainly searched by game fans.

Where to watch Mortal Kombat II

As a Warner Bros. film it streams on HBO Max after its digital rental window.
