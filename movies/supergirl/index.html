What is Supergirl about?

Kara Zor-El, Superman's cousin, sets out across the galaxy to hunt the villain who hurt her dog Krypto. It is a road-trip style space adventure and the second film in James Gunn and Peter Safran's new DC Universe, after Superman in 2025.

The story was announced as an adaptation of the comic Supergirl: Woman of Tomorrow by Tom King and Bilquis Evely.

Cast and crew

Milly Alcock, who first appeared as Kara in Superman, leads the film. Matthias Schoenaerts plays the villain, Eve Ridley co-stars, and Jason Momoa appears as Lobo. David Corenswet returns as Superman. Craig Gillespie, director of I, Tonya, directs from a script by Ana Nogueira.

Box office and reviews

Reviews were mixed and the film lost money, earning about $126 million against a budget of $170 to $186 million. People still search for it to keep up with the new DC Universe and to see how it sets up future films.

Where to watch Supergirl

As a Warner Bros. and DC Studios film it streams on HBO Max after its digital rental window.
