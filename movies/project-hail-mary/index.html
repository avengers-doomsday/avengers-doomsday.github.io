What is Project Hail Mary about?

Ryland Grace, a middle school science teacher, wakes up alone on a spacecraft far from Earth with no memory of how he got there. Slowly his memories return, and with them the reason for the mission: the Sun is dimming, and he may be humanity's last chance to save it.

It is based on the 2021 novel by Andy Weir, author of The Martian, and keeps the same mix of hard science, problem solving and humour.

Cast and crew

Ryan Gosling plays Grace and also produced the film. Sandra Hüller co-stars, with James Ortiz and Lionel Boyce. Phil Lord and Christopher Miller, the duo behind The Lego Movie and the Spider-Verse films, direct from a script by Drew Goddard, who also wrote The Martian. The spaceship interior was built as a real set in the UK.

Box office and reviews

Project Hail Mary got positive reviews and earned about $685 million worldwide, the seventh biggest film of the year and a rare original-style science fiction hit. Readers of the book keep searching to compare the two.
