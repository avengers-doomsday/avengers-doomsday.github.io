What is The Devil Wears Prada 2 about?

Twenty years after the first film, Runway magazine is in trouble. Print is shrinking, the media business has changed, and corporate threats are circling. Andy Sachs comes back into Miranda Priestly's world and ends up helping her fight for the magazine's survival.

The sequel keeps the sharp workplace comedy of the original but swaps assistant life for a story about power, money and staying relevant.

Cast and crew

All four leads are back: Meryl Streep as Miranda, Anne Hathaway as Andy, Emily Blunt as Emily and Stanley Tucci as Nigel. Kenneth Branagh, Lucy Liu and Justin Theroux join them. David Frankel directs again and Aline Brosh McKenna wrote it, the same team as in 2006. It was filmed in Manhattan and Milan.

Box office and reviews

Reviews were generally positive and it was a big hit, earning about $693 million on a $100 million budget, the sixth biggest film of 2026. Fashion and fan searches peaked around the premiere and keep coming as people discover it at home.

It is a 20th Century Studios film, so it streams on Disney+ (and on Hulu in the US) after its digital rental window.
