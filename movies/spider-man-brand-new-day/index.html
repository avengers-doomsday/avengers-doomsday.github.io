What is Spider-Man: Brand New Day about?

Spider-Man: Brand New Day is the fourth Marvel Cinematic Universe film with Tom Holland as Peter Parker and the first since No Way Home in 2021. That film ended with the whole world forgetting who Peter was, and this one shows him living with it: no Avengers on speed dial, no Ned or MJ in his corner, just a kid in New York working himself into the ground as a street-level hero.

The pressure starts to change his powers, and at the same time a new telepathic threat appears. The title comes from the 2008 comic storyline that reset Spider-Man's life, which fits the clean-slate idea of this film.

Cast and crew

Destin Daniel Cretton, who made Shang-Chi, directs from a script by Chris McKenna and Erik Sommers, the writers of the three earlier Holland films. Zendaya and Jacob Batalon are back, Sadie Sink joins in a new role, and two big MCU names cross over: Jon Bernthal as Frank Castle, the Punisher, and Mark Ruffalo as Bruce Banner. Florence Pugh, Marisa Tomei and Tramell Tillman are also in the cast.

Box office and reviews

It is the biggest film of 2026 by a wide margin. Since opening on July 31 it has taken about $2.52 billion worldwide, making it the highest-grossing Spider-Man film ever and one of the three biggest films of all time. Reviews were largely positive, with critics singling out Holland and the more grounded, personal story. Searches stay high because it is the most recent Marvel film before Avengers: Doomsday, and fans want to know how it connects.
