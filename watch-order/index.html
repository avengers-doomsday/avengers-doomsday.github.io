What to Watch Before Avengers: Doomsday

The 15 movies and series Disney+ recommends before December 18, in viewing order, with one line on why each one matters. Short on time? The six-title version is below.

  1. 1

    X-Men 2000

    Meet Professor X, Magneto, Cyclops, Mystique and the rest of the original mutant cast, several of whom return in Doomsday.

  2. 2

    X2 2003

    Nightcrawler joins and the original X-Men story continues with the same actors.

  3. 3

    Captain America: The First Avenger 2011

    Steve Rogers' origin and the first appearance of the Tesseract.

  4. 4

    The Avengers 2012

    The first team-up, and the first look at Thanos.

  5. 5

    Avengers: Infinity War 2018

    Thanos collects the Infinity Stones and wipes out half of all life.

  6. 6

    Avengers: Endgame 2019

    The Infinity Saga ends and the team changes for good. Doomsday picks up from this new line-up.

  7. 7

    Loki, Seasons 1 and 2 2021–2023

    The multiverse and the Time Variance Authority. This is the backbone of the whole multiverse story.

  8. 8

    Shang-Chi and the Legend of the Ten Rings 2021

    Introduces Shang-Chi, who is part of the Doomsday cast.

  9. 9

    Spider-Man: No Way Home 2021

    Characters from other universes cross into the MCU, the same idea Doomsday uses with the X-Men.

  10. 10

    Black Panther: Wakanda Forever 2022

    Shuri becomes Black Panther and Namor appears for the first time.

  11. 11

    Doctor Strange in the Multiverse of Madness 2022

    Alternate versions of familiar heroes, and how dangerous universe-hopping can be.

  12. 12

    Captain America: Brave New World 2025

    Sam Wilson as the new Captain America.

  13. 13

    Deadpool & Wolverine 2024

    The Fox-era X-Men world is folded into the MCU multiverse.

  14. 14

    Thunderbolts* 2025

    Yelena, Bucky, Red Guardian, U.S. Agent, Ghost and Bob come together.

  15. 15

    The Fantastic Four: First Steps 2025

    The Fantastic Four, and the first on-screen tease of Doctor Doom.

Only have a weekend? Watch these six

  1. Avengers: Endgame
  2. Loki, Seasons 1 and 2
  3. Spider-Man: No Way Home
  4. Deadpool & Wolverine
  5. Thunderbolts*
  6. The Fantastic Four: First Steps

Add X-Men and X2 if you want the background on the returning mutants.

How the list was put together

Disney+ published this viewing guide in August 2026 as preparation for the film. It mixes fourteen movies with one series, Loki, whose two seasons explain the multiverse and the Time Variance Authority that the new Avengers story is built on. The list starts with two films made by 20th Century Fox before the MCU existed, because many of their actors are back for Doomsday.

One film fans noticed is missing is The Marvels (2023), even though its ending connects to the X-Men. Watch it as a bonus if you have time.

Watch order FAQ

Do I need to watch every Marvel movie before Avengers: Doomsday?

No. The official Disney+ guide has 15 titles, and the six most important are Endgame, Loki, No Way Home, Deadpool & Wolverine, Thunderbolts* and The Fantastic Four: First Steps.

Why are the old X-Men movies on the list?

Patrick Stewart, Ian McKellen, James Marsden, Rebecca Romijn, Alan Cumming and other original X-Men actors are in the Doomsday cast, so X-Men (2000) and X2 introduce their characters.

Where can I stream these movies?

Most of the list is on Disney+, which published the viewing guide. Availability of the older X-Men films can differ by country.
